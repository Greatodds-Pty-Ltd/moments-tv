# Hero image library: generation prompt

Make one image per sport and reuse it for every Priority A moment in that sport. The TV page writes the event name, date, campaign line and countdown on top of the image.

## Prompt (swap in the scene)
Create a 1324×1188 background image for an internal office display.
Scene: [SCENE]
Photoreal, cinematic, dramatic stadium lighting.
Colour grade dominated by deep royal blue (#0A5EEA fading to navy #061F3F), with one small red accent (#F13131).
Keep the left half of the frame calm and dark (sky, haze or shadow) with no detail, so text can sit on top.
No text, words or numbers. No logos, team crests, kit badges, sponsor marks or recognisable players.
